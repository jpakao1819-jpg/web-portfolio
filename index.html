User access structure

User type Access level
System administrator Full access to all branches, departments, users, files, tasks, reports, and settings
Executive / Senior management Organisation-wide access to approved reports and operational information
Branch manager Access to the assigned branch and its employees
Department manager Access to the assigned department and department tasks
Employee Access to personal tasks and approved department information
Client / Public visitor Access only to public product, service, and advertising pages
Example: The marketing manager can view and manage marketing-related work. Employees in marketing see only permitted information and tasks. The administrator and authorised senior management see the overall organisation across all branches and departments.
